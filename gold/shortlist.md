# Gold shortlist

Clips that passed a visual check on Oct 17, 2026: real clay on screen in the measured segment, no third-party characters, no titles or 2D sections in the segment used. Licenses and authors are in `public_catalog.csv`; measurements in `measurements.csv`; what they show in research/16.

**Used in R&D:** every clip below was measured and looked at during the Oct 17 pass. Treat all of them as tuning material. Draw the held-out slice from catalog clips that are not on this list.

Start time is where the measured segment begins (seconds); segments run about 24 seconds.
